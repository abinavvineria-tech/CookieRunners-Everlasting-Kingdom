The Garden Awaits

Quiet anticipation. A welcome is being prepared, and memories of friends fill the air.

The garden has been tended through every season of waiting. Soft soil, open gates, a place set at the table — everything ready for the day White Lily Cookie steps back into the light. It is a gentle thing, this kind of hope: not loud, not hurried, simply steady.

Friends remember her in their own ways. A shared promise. A quiet joke. A moment under the same moon. Each memory is a bud waiting to open.

November 5, 2026

Welcome Home

The reunion. Friends gathering. A hopeful ending about togetherness.

The day has come. White Lily Cookie steps into the garden, and the friends who love her are already there — Pure Vanilla Cookie with a gentle smile, the Ancient Heroes standing side by side as they once did, Mogra Cookie buzzing with joy, Hollyberry Cookie holding the gate open wide.

There are no grand speeches, only quiet laughter and the rustle of petals in the breeze. Years fold into moments, and the garden remembers what time cannot erase.

Togetherness is not the absence of distance — it is the promise that, no matter how long the road, there will always be a garden waiting, and friends ready to say: welcome home.
