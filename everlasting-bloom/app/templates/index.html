EVERLASTING BLOOM

A Tribute to White Lily Cookie

A respectful fan-made digital garden celebrating White Lily Cookie — Ancient Hero — and her enduring friendship with Pure Vanilla Cookie. May this tribute honor the values of kindness, friendship, respect, courage, and hope.

Welcome to the Garden

EVERLASTING BLOOM is an unofficial fan tribute dedicated to White Lily Cookie from Cookie Run: Kingdom. Here we honor her journey — not through fabricated canon, but through verified milestones, community memories, and the quiet beauty of a flower that never truly fades.

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by Devsisters. Cookie Run trademarks belong to their respective owners.
