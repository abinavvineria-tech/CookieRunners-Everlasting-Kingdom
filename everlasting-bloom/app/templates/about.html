Project Purpose

EVERLASTING BLOOM exists as a respectful, unofficial digital tribute to White Lily Cookie — Ancient Hero — from Cookie Run: Kingdom. It celebrates her verified story, her friendship with Pure Vanilla Cookie, and the enduring values of kindness, friendship, respect, courage, and hope.

This is not an official Devsisters product. All Cookie Run trademarks belong to their respective owners. No unverified official lore is presented as fact.

Credits & Acknowledgments

Built with Python Flask, SQLite, HTML5, modern CSS, and JavaScript. Design follows a cinematic botanical aesthetic with glassmorphism, pearl white, lavender, and subtle gold tones.

Created by a member of the Cookie Runners community from Tamil Nadu, with deep respect for White Lily Cookie's legacy and the values she embodies.

Technical Details

  • • Mobile-first responsive design
  • • SQLite persistence with secure validation
  • • Admin authentication for moderation
  • • CLI companion: bloomctl
  • • No fabricated official lore or unverified dates
  • • Works locally on Termux and Ubuntu
