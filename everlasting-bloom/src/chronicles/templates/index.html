About This Archive

Identity & Purpose

This archive is an unofficial fan-made digital museum — not affiliated with or endorsed by Devsisters. It separates verified franchise history from personal journey milestones and clearly labels fan-created content. Historical dates come from user-verified references or are marked uncertain to avoid inventing official timelines. Personal achievements (Eternal Sugar Cookie, Timekeeper Cookie, White Lily / Awakened White Lily memories, B!TES Fan Contest, Collector title, Rajapendra Cookie) are documented as personal milestones — never presented as official game events.

Every interactive element performs a real action: navigation links load content, the timeline filters by year/category/source/search, events can be added and edited, and all changes persist in SQLite.
