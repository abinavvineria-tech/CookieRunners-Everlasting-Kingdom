# White Lily Cookie — Eternal Remembrance

*An unofficial CookieVerse fan tribute (not Devsisters)*

## Legend
Ancient Hero; journey = truth, sacrifice, courage, friendship, hope.

## Official Quotes (Canon)
- “I know what must be done.”
- “I will never forgive what I've done.”

## Eight Principles of White Lily’s Light
1. Truth guides every step, even when the path is dark.
2. Sacrifice is made not for glory, but for those we love.
3. Courage blooms in the heart that refuses to yield.
4. Friendship is the root that steadies the stem through storms.
5. Hope is the seed that never ceases to sprout anew.
6. Honor is kept in silence when words would break the vow.
7. Redemption grows from accepting the past, not denying it.
8. Eternity is found in the moments we share, not the span of years.

*May her petals forever remind us to walk with integrity and compassion.*
