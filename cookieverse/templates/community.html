About the Community

The Cookie Runners Golden Ballon d'Or is open to all fans who wish to celebrate Cookie Run characters and moments. Nominations are welcome from anyone, and the first edition's predefined winners — White Lily Cookie, Mogra Cookie, and Pure Vanilla Cookie — will always be preserved. Additional editions may expand winners and categories as the community grows.
